        Left-drag turns or moves the view (the switch above; hold Shift for the other one), right-drag always moves it; the wheel, a two-finger swipe or + / − zoom; the arrow keys move it once the view has the focus. On a touch screen: one finger turns or moves, two fingers move and pinch to zoom. Coordinates in µm.

        The vessels are thinned to their centrelines, which become a network of branches between junctions and ends; short spurs and specks are pruned. A branch cut by a face of the stack is counted but truncated. Radii are the distance from the centreline to the wall: within half a voxel, thin vessels slightly wide and thick ones slightly narrow. Where a vessel touches the bone, the mask bulges and adds junctions that are not forks. The files (network.json, branches.csv, centrelines.npz) are in the segmentation folder; the 3D tab draws the centrelines.
